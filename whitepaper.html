1. Abstract & Vision

$BANKRUPT (BKT) is a decentralized engagement layer constructed for the Robinhood Chain ecosystem. 3. Proof-of-Engagement (PoE) Mining

To maintain long-term supply stability across high user traffic, $BKT mining relies on strict structural rules:

  • 7-Day Streak Loop: Daily check-ins scale earnings up to Day 7. After completing Day 7, the streak cycle resets to Day 1.
  • Dynamic Halving Engine: As active user counts increase globally, mining yield dynamically adjusts downward to safeguard token supply longevity.
  • Quest Verification: On-chain and social tasks provide one-time boost allocations from the mining reserve.
  • Capped Referral Protocol: Users are assigned a strict limit of 10 referrals to prevent sybil attacks, with custom allocations reserved for verified creators.
